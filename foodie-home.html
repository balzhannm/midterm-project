About the Foodie Project

Foodie is a student web project (Midterm) — a concept website for a home-style restaurant. The target audience is city residents looking for a cozy place for lunch or dinner. The goal of the site is to showcase the menu, share the restaurant's atmosphere, and let guests book a table online.
